1. Qué datos guardamos

Michi Fly no te pide cuenta, nombre, correo ni ningún dato personal. Todo tu progreso (racha del bono diario, récord del reto del día, fichas y skins de la sesión) se guarda únicamente en tu dispositivo y nunca sale de él hacia nuestros servidores — no tenemos servidores.

Para mostrarlos, Google puede recoger identificadores del dispositivo (como el ID de publicidad de Android), dirección IP, ubicación aproximada, información sobre tu interacción con los anuncios y datos de diagnóstico y rendimiento de la app, y puede usarlos para mostrarte publicidad personalizada o no personalizada según tus ajustes.

3. Menores

No recogemos deliberadamente datos personales de nadie, incluidos menores de 13 años. Los anuncios los sirve Google conforme a sus propias políticas.

Privacy Policy — Michi Fly

App: Michi Fly (com.michifly.game) · Developer: Dylshan Studio · Effective: October 2026

1. What data we store

Michi Fly asks for no account, name, email or any personal data. All your progress (daily bonus streak, daily challenge best, session tokens and skins) is stored only on your device and never leaves it for our servers — we have no servers.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To serve them, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location, information about your interaction with ads and app diagnostics and performance data, and may use them to show you personalized or non-personalized ads depending on your settings.

Learn more:

  • How Google uses information for advertising
  • AdMob privacy policy
  • How to control the ads you see (you can reset your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads)

3. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13. Ads are served by Google under its own policies.

4. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, write to: supportmichifly@gmail.com

5. Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version here with its effective date.
